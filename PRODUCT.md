# Ooh Jay Product Context

## Product

Ooh Jay is a plumbing company for homes and businesses in Nigeria & beyond. It delivers water infrastructure, wet-area installations, plant-room work, diagnostics, servicing, and reliable plumbing execution. Building construction is an additional service, not the company's primary identity.

## Audience

- Homeowners who need dependable residential plumbing work
- Business owners, property managers, and commercial teams
- Developers, architects, consultants, and contractors who need a dependable plumbing partner

Visitors may be evaluating a home plumbing job, a commercial plumbing system, a bathroom or wet area, a plant room, a retrofit, ongoing maintenance, or an additional construction project.

## Primary job of the website

Help a prospective client quickly understand Ooh Jay's plumbing services, trust the quality of the work, see relevant evidence, and begin a quote conversation. Construction should be presented as a useful secondary capability.

The site is a persuasion and qualification surface. It should make contacting the company feel like the obvious next step without becoming aggressive or vague.

## Honest proof available

- Detailed residential and commercial plumbing capabilities
- Additional building construction capability
- Project records with locations, scope, years, and measurable specifications
- Plumbing and construction imagery
- Process and delivery information
- Direct WhatsApp, phone, and email contact

Do not invent client logos, awards, certifications, completion percentages, testimonials, or project outcomes that are not supplied.

## Brand character

Ooh Jay should feel:

- Capable and technically serious
- Calm under complex site conditions
- Clear about scope and process
- Proud of finished work without exaggeration
- Local in knowledge and professional in delivery
- Approachable for a homeowner and credible to a business

It should not feel like:

- A generic home-services template
- An engineering dashboard
- An architecture magazine
- A construction company pretending to be a design agency
- A loud construction advert

## Content voice

Use plain, specific, confident language. Prefer real scope, materials, locations, and outcomes over broad claims. Keep technical detail available for people who need it, but make the first read understandable to a non-specialist client.

## Experience priorities

1. Establish Ooh Jay as a residential and commercial plumbing company in Nigeria & beyond within the first viewport.
2. Show credible work and relevant service areas early (Nigeria & beyond, not Lagos & Abuja only).
3. Make the quote/contact path visible without dominating the page.
4. Let users scan services, projects, process, and proof on mobile.
5. Preserve access to technical detail through progressive disclosure.

## Reach (hardened 2026-09-07)

Operates in Nigeria & beyond. All "Lagos & Abuja" positioning has been generalized. Project-specific locations (e.g., Lekki, Ikoyi) remain as factual project metadata; brand-level claims use "Nigeria & beyond".
