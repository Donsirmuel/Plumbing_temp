About OOH JAY

OOH JAY delivers high-performance residential estates, commercial hydronics, and luxury wet areas across Lagos, Abuja, and nationwide across Nigeria. All structural and MEP engineering is executed by COREN-registered engineers operating under BS 8110, Eurocode 2, and strict WHO potable water quality guidelines.

Core Capabilities & Services

01. Structural Construction & Civil Engineering

Turnkey commercial and high-end residential structural engineering, specialized deep foundation piling, post-tensioned concrete floor slabs for expansive open spans, fair-faced architectural concrete, and subterranean tanking waterproofing systems.

Technical Standards: ±1.5mm Laser Verified Optical Tolerance, 28-day concrete strength protocol, BS 8110 and Eurocode 2 compliance.

02. Plumbing & Precision Hydronic Systems

Engineered potable water distribution networks, phosphorus-deoxidised brazed copper manifolds, multilayer PEX pipework, borehole filtration and reverse osmosis, constant-pressure variable-speed booster pumps (IE4 high efficiency), and silent acoustic drainage (<18 dB).

Technical Standards: Mandatory 16-bar hydrostatic pressure test held for 24 hours prior to wall or floor closure.

03. Architectural Finishing & Luxury Wet Areas

Installation of concealed thermostatic fixtures, rainfall shower heads, wall-hung sanitaryware, book-matched marble and travertine cladding, zero-threshold trench drains, and dual-membrane tanking sealants.

04. Renovation & Plant Room MEP

Non-destructive ultrasonic acoustic leak detection, thermal imaging diagnostics, commercial sewage treatment plants (STP), central boilers, and under-2-hour emergency response across Lagos.
