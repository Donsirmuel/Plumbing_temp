About OOH JAY

OOH JAY is a plumbing company for homes and businesses in Nigeria & beyond. We install, repair and maintain water systems, bathrooms and wet areas, plant rooms and drainage — with construction support when a project needs it.

Core Capabilities & Services

01. Plumbing & Water Systems

Water supply networks, brazed copper and PPR pipework, booster pumps, water treatment and drainage. Every concealed line is pressure-tested before walls are closed.

02. Bathrooms & Wet Areas

Bathrooms, wet rooms, concealed mixers, waterproofing and sanitaryware — carefully installed for homes and commercial properties.

03. Plant Rooms, Diagnostics & Servicing

Plant rooms, leak diagnostics, servicing and ongoing maintenance to keep water systems running as they should.

04. Construction Support

Building support when a plumbing project needs it — planned together with the plumbing so structure and services work as one.
